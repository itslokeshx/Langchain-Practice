Virat Kohli

Virat Kohli is an Indian international cricketer and one of the greatest batsmen in the history of cricket. He was born on November 5, 1988, in Delhi, India.

Kohli made his international debut for India in 2008 in a One Day International (ODI) match against Sri Lanka. He became the captain of the Indian cricket team across all formats and is known for his aggressive leadership and exceptional consistency.

Virat Kohli is a right-handed batsman. He has scored more than 80 international centuries across Test, ODI, and T20I cricket, making him one of the highest century scorers in the history of the sport.

He played a crucial role in India's victory in the 2011 ICC Cricket World Cup and was also part of the team that won the 2013 ICC Champions Trophy.

Kohli has represented Royal Challengers Bengaluru (RCB) in the Indian Premier League (IPL) since the league began in 2008. He is the highest run scorer in IPL history and holds the record for the most runs in a single IPL season, scoring 973 runs in 2016.

He is known for his fitness and transformed the fitness culture of Indian cricket. Many young cricketers consider him an inspiration because of his discipline and work ethic.

Virat Kohli married Bollywood actress Anushka Sharma in December 2017. They have two children.

Achievements:
- ICC Cricketer of the Year multiple times.
- ICC ODI Player of the Year.
- Arjuna Award recipient.
- Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna Award recipient.
- Padma Shri recipient.

Playing Style:
Virat Kohli is famous for chasing targets successfully in ODI cricket. Because of his remarkable performances while chasing, he is often called the "Chase Master." His strengths include cover drives, quick running between the wickets, and the ability to build innings under pressure.
